Privatier: first start
======================

1. Drag Privatier onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Privatier from Applications (double-click).

3. The first time, your Mac says it cannot check the app, because Privatier is not signed by Apple yet.
   This is expected and the app is safe to open. Click "Done" (do not click "Move to Trash"). Then do this once:

   - Open the Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   - Scroll down to the section "Security". You see: "Privatier was blocked to protect your Mac".
   - Click "Open Anyway", enter your Mac password (or use Touch ID), and click "Open".

From then on Privatier opens like any other app. You do not need to do this again.

Good to know
- Privatier can only see the folders you choose yourself (with the Choose-folder button or by dropping a folder onto the
  window). macOS enforces this, so it cannot look at your other files. Choose your folder again if a message says access
  is missing.
- Privatier works completely offline. The AI that finds names is part of the app. Your files never leave your Mac.
- The first start takes a little longer than usual (about half a minute): the AI is loaded.
- Needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer on a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer).
